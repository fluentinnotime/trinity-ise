Task 3 — Reading into writing

"Write an article for your school magazine (100-130 words) about the relationship teenagers have with music. Use the information you read in Task 2 to:"

  • explain what types of music teenagers like today
  • suggest how teenagers can benefit from music and
  • say how the ways of listening to music have changed.
